# Incident response

Severities: P1 critical platform/security outage; P2 major degraded recruitment operation; P3 limited customer impact; P4 minor issue.

For P1/P2: assign incident commander, operations lead and communications owner; open an incident record; preserve logs/evidence; establish impact and tenant scope; mitigate before root-cause work; update stakeholders on the agreed cadence; and record decision timestamps.

Security incidents must notify the named security contact and privacy owner immediately where personal data may be affected. Regulatory notification decisions are made by the authorised privacy/legal owner, not automated by the platform.

Close only after service is stable, data integrity is reconciled and follow-up actions have owners/dates. P1/P2 require a blameless postmortem.
