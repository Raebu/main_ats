# Service ownership

Each production deployment unit must have a primary owner, secondary owner, escalation channel and security/privacy contact recorded in the production environment configuration.

Logical ownership:
- edge-api: Platform Engineering
- recruitment-core: Talent Platform
- selection: Talent Platform
- experience: Candidate Experience / Platform
- platform-control: Platform Engineering / Security
- intelligence-data: Data & Platform
- malware-scanner: Platform Security

The production proof must identify real people or an active on-call rotation before Stage 12 can pass. Repository labels are not a substitute for an operational owner.
